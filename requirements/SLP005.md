# SLP005 — Atomic Sleep State Bit-Field Update

## Statement

The 4-bit sleep-state field (FWK014) shall be updated atomically before and after each sleep transition to allow accurate resume after power loss.

## Rationale

Formalises the bit-field mechanism referenced in FWK014.

## Scope

All scripts and shared libraries in the framework.

## Implications

- Non-compliance must be corrected before merge.
