# NET011 — NIC Include/Exclude Lists

## Statement

net_test.sh shall accept include/exclude lists (_net_test_include_pairs and _net_test_exclude_pairs in config) so operators can declare exactly which NIC pairs to test. NICs not in the include list (or present in the exclude list) shall not be moved into namespaces.

## Rationale

Resolves BUG0015. Without this, operators cannot prevent net_test from consuming the SSH lifeline NIC on remotely-managed DUTs.

## Scope

All scripts and shared libraries in the framework.

## Implications

- Non-compliance must be corrected before merge.
