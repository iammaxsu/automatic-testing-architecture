# SLP002 — Configurable Pre-Sleep Delay

## Statement

The delay before initiating a sleep transition shall be configurable (_sleep_delay_after_boot, default 30 s).

## Rationale

See statement.

## Scope

All scripts and shared libraries in the framework.
