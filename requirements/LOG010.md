# LOG010 — Elapsed Time Reporting

## Statement

The elapsed wall-clock time shall be reported in the main log (format Elapsed: HH:MM:SS). When result.json is produced (per LOG015), it shall additionally include elapsed_seconds: <int> and elapsed_human: <string> fields.

## Rationale

Implements the reporting half of FUN003 (measurement requirement).

## Scope

All scripts and shared libraries in the framework.

## Implications

- Non-compliance must be corrected before merge.
