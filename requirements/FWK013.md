# FWK013 — Resume After Power Cycle

## Statement

After an unplanned power cycle or interruption, the framework shall automatically resume an incomplete test from the last completed step. Resume granularity is at the loop level (_n in counter file). Partially completed loops are restarted from the beginning of that loop.

## Rationale

Resume granularity clarified after Ansible verification.

## Scope

All scripts and shared libraries in the framework.

## Implications

- Non-compliance must be corrected before merge.
