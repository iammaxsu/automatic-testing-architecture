# PWR001 — AT and ATX PSU Type Selection

## Statement

The power cycle test shall support two PSU types, selectable via `--type ATX|AT` (default from config.py):

- **ATX PSU**: relay simulates a momentary power-button press on the ATX header.
  - Short press (≤ 1 s, configurable): powers DUT on or requests soft shutdown.
  - Long press (≥ 4 s, configurable): forces power off regardless of OS state.
- **AT PSU**: relay acts as a maintained power switch.
  - Relay closed = AC power flowing (DUT on).
  - Relay open = AC power cut (DUT off).

The PSU type shall determine which relay operations are called for each phase of the boot cycle.

## Rationale

AT and ATX power delivery mechanisms are fundamentally different. AT PSUs cut AC power directly; ATX PSUs require a momentary signal on the PS_ON# line. Using a momentary pulse for AT testing (the original mistake in `gpio_rasp_unittest.py`) produces incorrect behaviour — the relay closes and opens before the PSU latches, resulting in no actual state change.

## Scope

`src/python/relay.py` and `src/python/power_cycle.py`.

## Implications

- `relay.py` must expose separate primitives: `atx_press()`, `atx_force_off()`, `at_power_on()`, `at_power_off()`.
- The calling code in `power_cycle.py` must select the correct primitive based on `--type`.

## Verification

Code Review: confirm `relay.py` implements both maintained-state (AT) and momentary-pulse (ATX) primitives; confirm `power_cycle.py` dispatches correctly.
