# NET016 — NIC Error / Discard Counter Verification

## Statement

For each (interface, speed) under test the framework shall snapshot the NIC
hardware error and discard counters immediately before and immediately after
the throughput runs, and record the delta:

- receive errors, transmit errors,
- receive discards, transmit discards,

for both NICs in the pair. A non-zero **error** delta (receive or transmit)
shall be reported in the verdict reason (NET008) and, when configured to do so
(`_net_err_fail_on_delta`), shall downgrade an otherwise-PASS speed verdict to
FAIL. Discard deltas are reported but, being softer, do not fail the verdict by
default.

## Rationale

A link can meet its throughput threshold (NET009) while still silently
corrupting or dropping frames — a marginal cable, a failing PHY, or EMI will
increment the NIC's CRC/error counters without necessarily collapsing average
throughput. A Mbps-only verdict misses exactly the class of intermittent
hardware faults this framework exists to catch. Reading the counters that the
hardware/driver already maintain (e.g. `Get-NetAdapterStatistics` on Windows,
`ethtool -S` on Linux) costs nothing extra and is standard practice in
industrial NIC validation.

## Scope

All scripts and shared libraries in the framework.

## Implications

1. Error/discard counters are captured before and after each speed's runs and
   the delta is recorded in result.json.
2. A non-zero error delta is surfaced in the rendered report and the verdict
   reason.
3. Whether a non-zero error delta fails the verdict is configurable (SET001).
4. Non-compliance must be corrected before merge.
