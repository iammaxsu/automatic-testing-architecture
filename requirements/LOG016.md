# LOG016 — Log Rotation Policy

## Statement

Log rotation or archiving shall be defined: logs older than N days (configurable) shall be compressed or deleted.

## Rationale

Without this, the logs/ directory grows unboundedly on long-running test beds.

## Scope

All scripts and shared libraries in the framework.

## Implications

- Non-compliance must be corrected before merge.
